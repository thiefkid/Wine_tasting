How the ranking works

Prestige blends three things: estimated bottle price (50%), how famous the producer or wine is (35%), and the James Suckling score printed on the official cheat sheet (15%). Fame tiers run from Icon (a first-call name like Pavie, Ornellaia or Almaviva) down to Discovery.

Prices are my estimates of current retail per 750 ml bottle, based on merchant listings and release prices as of October 2026. Treat them as a guide for ranking, not a quote. JS points come from the event's own wine list.
